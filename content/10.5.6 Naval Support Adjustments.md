### 10.5.6 Naval Support Adjustments

Naval Support Adjustments for:

- Cargo  Handling  –  Plus  5  for  each  undamaged  naval  support

device present.

- Rearm Level – Plus 5 (Plus 1 in civilian ports) for each undamaged

naval support device present.

- Repairs  –  100  undamaged  naval  support  devices  approximately equals a small AR repair ship, and 50 undamaged naval support devices approximately equals small tender.
