###### 6.1.2.2.1.2 Meet TF

The Meet TF option allows a TF to meet another selected TF in open water.  To set TF 16 to meet another TF, click the arrow to the left of this title. The screen will be replaced by a large display of the tactical map. Scroll around and click on a TF for the current TF to meet. The player may choose subsequent actions from a list of options: Meet then: Refuel the TF, or Merge with the TF. This option is especially useful for replenishment TFs.
