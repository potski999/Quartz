### 4.2.1 Terrain

Terrain on the Tactical Map is represented by both hexes and hex sides. Both are used to represent various types of land and ocean terrain. Hotkey [D] toggles on/off a text description for each hex, while hotkey [S] toggles on/off hex side colors which depict different hex side details. See sections 4.2.1.3 and 4.2.1.4.
