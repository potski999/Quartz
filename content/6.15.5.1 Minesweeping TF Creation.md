#### 6.15.5.1 Minesweeping TF Creation

Minesweeping capable ships may be placed in Minesweeping or Local Minesweeping TF and can sweep a minefield by having minesweeping orders for a given location for the minefield to be swept.  If a Minesweeping TF is clearing mines in a hex that is close to coastal guns, the TF may be fired on.
