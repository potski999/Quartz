### 14.2.4 Merchant Shipyard

Produces civilian ships and some smaller military ships like sub chasers, patrol boats, minesweepers and other auxiliary military vessels that are not as complex as larger, armored combat ships.

Each one will provide 400 tons of space for producing civilian or very small military patrol craft.

The consumption per day under construction depends on the ship and the speed of construction.

Note: Naval and merchant shipyards are interchangeable. You can turn free capacity of one type into the other type. The difference is in the kind of ships they can build as well as the cost per ton of ship that is constructed.

For a shipyard it does not matter if it builds a 6,000-ton cruiser or Cargo Ship. Just the type of material input is different for naval or civilian type constructions.
