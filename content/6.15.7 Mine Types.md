### 6.15.7 Mine Types

War  in  Spain  1936-39  has  2  general  types  of  mines.  Contact  mines and  magnetic  influence  mines.  Mines  with  a  penetration  value  of  10 are handled as magnetic mines. Some mine clearing ships have special equipment to deal with magnetic mines. Without this special equipment magnetic mines are hard to clear. Ships made of wood are immune to magnetic mines.
