## 16.7 Arrivals at Enemy Occupied Bases

The rules for ships, air groups and land-based units (collectively known as items) coming onto the map, when the assigned base is controlled by the enemy, are as follows:

- Ground units will arrive at the national capital hex, ships will arrive at the national Navy HQ hex.  If these locations are not under control of their side, the arrival will not happen at all. - Some ground units may be set to only appear when the target location is under control.  This applies to various local militia mostly.  These units can only be formed when the location is controlled. If the location is not controlled at time of arrival, those units will not be created at all.
