## 16.6 Ground Units

Bases that are “connected” may always serve to access the pool of devices for their nation only. The chosen Supply Base the unit can reach over land counts for a given unit. Depending on the replacement model, you may have to move either supply, pool points or devices themselves into range of the units asking for replacements.

Destroyed units can be recalled into existence from the destroyed ground units screen in the Intelligence window. If a parent is destroyed, one of the fragments (if there are any) will become the new parent. Destroyed fragments will have their TOE added back to their parent unit. Units can be recalled at the capital or a navy HQ. These bases do not need to be connected for this. If a nation no longer haves one of these bases under control, recalling is not possible.
