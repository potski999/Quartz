## 17.3 Defeat, Surrender and

Homefront Morale Every nation has a tolerance level against loss of lives of their population, soldiers, pilots, and ship crews. When soldiers die, Cities and Bases get bombed or even fall to the enemy, this will cause loss of life to the native owner. You can lose morale by bombing a native City that you lost to the enemy. The current state of the nation’s morale can be seen on a bar on  the  bottom  of  the  nation  information  screen.  See  section  5.1.  The indicator bar will show various icons for you to see how close you are to the various penalties.
