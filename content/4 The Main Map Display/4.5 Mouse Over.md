## 4.5 Mouse Over

When the mouse cursor is moved over an icon on the Tactical Map (excluding the Minefields icons mentioned in [[4.2.2.6 Task Force Icons|section 4.2.2.6]]), a summary of information relating to the icon appears in a pop-up window. Mouse Over displayed information on player owned icons is accurate but the information displayed on enemy icons is both more limited and the accuracy is dependent on the current Maximum Detection Level on that enemy icon. (see [[10 Bases|Section 10.0]])
