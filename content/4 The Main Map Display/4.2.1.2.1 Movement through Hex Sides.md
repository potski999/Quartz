##### 4.2.1.2.1 Movement through Hex Sides

In *War in Spain 1936-39*, movement is across hex sides, not along hex boundaries. The color of a hex side (hotkey \[S\]) indicates whether land units and/or ships can cross that hex side. The four hex side colors and the units which can cross these hex sides are:

- **Blue** – Ocean/Lake hex side. Naval units only.
- **Green** – Land hex side. Ground units only.
- **Red** – Impassable hex side. No Ground or Naval units.
- **White** – Land and Ocean hex sides. Both Ground and Naval units may pass.
