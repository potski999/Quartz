### 4.5.3 Airfield Icon

Below is the Mouse Over information provided by pointing the mouse cursor at a player owned Airfield Icon.

From top to bottom, the information displayed is as follows:

- On the airfield there are a total of 4 CASA SM.62 bis patrol aircraft. The maximum size of this group at this base is 5.

Below is the Mouse Over Information provided by pointing the mouse cursor at a player owned air group on the hex interface panel.

From top to bottom the information given is as follows:

- The unit name.
- The number of aircraft in the group, group size and the airframe type.
- Information on active, grounded and damaged aircraft.
- The current primary mission and target.
- The current secondary mission.
- The mission percentages. In this case, 100% of the active planes will conduct the primary mission.
