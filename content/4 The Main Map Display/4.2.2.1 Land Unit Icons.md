#### 4.2.2.1 Land Unit Icons

The major nations in *War in Spain 1936-39* are:

**Nationalis**t: Germany, Italy and Nationalist Spain. Their flag has a blue frame around it.

**Republicans**: USSR, Republican Spain and Republic of the North. Their flag has a red frame around it.
