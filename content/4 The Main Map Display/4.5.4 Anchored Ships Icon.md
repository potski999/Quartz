### 4.5.4 Anchored Ships Icon

This is the Mouse Over information provided by pointing the mouse cursor at a player owned Anchored Ships Icon. On the top you will see a list of ship types present at the port. The numbers in parenthesis are the number of ships conducting repairs in modes other than readiness.

Below this you’ll find the types and number of any aircraft located on the ships. In this case there are not any aircraft on the ships.
