#### 4.2.1.4 Maritime Transportation Links 

There are three different Maritime Transportation Link types; two are found on the Tactical Map and the third is found only in the “off map” areas. The “on map” types derive their special qualities from their hex sides whereas for the third, standard naval movement is changed slightly due to the special features of “off map” areas.
