#### 4.2.1.6 Off Map Maritime Transport Links

These are discussed in greater detail in [[6.3 TF Off-Map Movement|section 6.3]] but are mentioned here so that their map art can be identified.

**Transit zone** – Sea connection found only in the “off map” area. Connects off map bases to other off map bases. Allows Task Forces to travel directly between two connected off map bases.

**Holding box** – Location of Task Forces travelling “off map”. Task Forces moving to or from an “off map” base remain in the Holding box until they complete that part of their travel.
