### 2.2.5 Loading a Save Game

Clicking on this button will open the saved game screen. Just select your save and click the load button. Loading a PBEM or WEGO save will give the player a few options. See sections 3.2 and 3.3 for details.

The exit game button will, when clicked, exits *War in Spain 1936-39*, and returns you to your desktop.
