## 13.1 Weather Details

Offensive Air Missions can be aborted after all preparations have been made, but prior to take-off, due to bad weather over the air unit’s base or over the intended target. The aircraft will not fly another Mission during that Air Strike phase. The weather at each base or target is determined by  each  air  phase  and  is  not  linked  to  prior  weather  at  that  location. Each  Base  and  Target  hex  is  checked  each  air  phase  for  its  weather, independent of all other hexes.

The  Weather  levels  from  best  to  worst  are  clear,  light  cloud,  Light Rain,  overcast,  Heavy  Rain,  thunderstorms  and  severe  storms.  Each weather level will come with wind speeds defined in the Beaufort Scale.
