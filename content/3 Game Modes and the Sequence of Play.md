# 3 Game Modes and the Sequence of Play

In War in Spain 1936-39 there are 4 modes of play. They each have a different sequence of play which is followed by each one. The 4 modes of play are:

- AI Games – One player, (Nationalist or Republican), vs the computer, (Nationalist or Republican). - PBEM Games – Two players vs each other. - Head-to-Head Games – Two players vs each other using the same computer.

- WEGO Team Games – Three to six players (two teams) vs each other.
