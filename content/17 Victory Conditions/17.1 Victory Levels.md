## 17.1 Victory Levels

*War in Spain 1936-39* can end in a Decisive, Major, Minor or Tactical Victory for one side, or in a draw. Victory Levels are displayed at the end of the game and determined as follows:

- **Decisive Victory** – VP Score is 6 times (or greater) higher than the enemy’s VP Score.

- **Major Victory** – VP Score is at least 4 times higher than the enemy’s VP Score.

- **Minor Victory** – VP Score is at least 2 times higher than the enemy’s VP Score.

- **Tactical Victory** – VP Score is at least a bit higher than the enemy’s VP Score.

- **Draw** – The Republican VP Score and Nationalist VP Score are equal.
