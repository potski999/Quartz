## 19.7 Ground Combat

During ground combat, there is a chance to break through the enemy lines.  This may happen due to superior firepower, normally later in combat, or earlier by a lucky engagement. This means attacking a group of devices that cannot counter the attackers. An example of this is an attack by tanks versus devices that failed in a check for AT support nearby. Whatever the cause, a breakthrough increases firepower for the unit that has broken through. Further, attacking units held in reserve may become active to exploit a breach. Defending units in reserve may react to seal a breach. Reserve units will only activate into combat upon these events.
