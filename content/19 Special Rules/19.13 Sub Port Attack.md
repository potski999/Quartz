## 19.13 Sub Port Attack

The mission “Submarine Port Attack” unleashes those midget subs or “Majale” nuisance craft for port attack.  Any submarine can attack anchored ships if the target hex has a port smaller than size 3 and the port is not a natural protected anchorage. But this kind of direct attack will reveal the subs position and have port ASW assets try to retaliate. A

“Submarine Port Attack” TF with lowest threat tolerance is meant to only do periscope photo reconnaissance of the Port and it’s anchored ships.
