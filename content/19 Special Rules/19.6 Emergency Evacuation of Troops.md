## 19.6 Emergency Evacuation of Troops

It is possible to pick up troops with any type of ship.  The troop and cargo space are derived from the ship size in that case, much like it is with rescue of troops from sinking ships by other ships nearby.  The TF type to be used is “ESCORT”. This type of mission is an emergency evacuation, and the troops will suffer massive disruption and other side effects. This only works with the “Pickup Troops” command. Normal loading of troops will not use this regardless of TF type.
