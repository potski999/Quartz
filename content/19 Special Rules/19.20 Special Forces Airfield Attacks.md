## 19.20 Special Forces Airfield Attacks

When special forces (Like the LRDG) attack hexes with aircraft, there is a good chance they will damage or destroy a good number of aircraft. This can be achieved by using any offensive device in the ground unit. Hit and run attacks by those ground units can do considerable damage to the aircraft of the enemy.
