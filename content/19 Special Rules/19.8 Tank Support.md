## 19.8 Tank Support

If either side has tank support, and the opponent AT devices (including tanks) cannot effectively engage all of them, there is a bonus to infantry of the attacking side. Thus, having tank support can increase firepower above what the tanks contribute by themselves, also meaning that even weak tanks can give this bonus as long as the opponent AT power cannot harm them. Both superior numbers as well as sufficient Penetration of the opposing devices is considered. Outnumbering AT guns is as good as having few, but invulnerable tanks.
