## 19.5 Beaching Ships

Ships that are “sunk” while disbanded are not removed from game. Ships preforming actions in port (loading/ unloading) may also be considered sunk in the harbor bay and thus not removed from the game. They can be refloated by the Port or repair ships even out of port. Beaching a ship can save it for the moment but be aware that beached ships will fall into enemy hands if hostile land forces take control of that hex. Italian CM Taranto was sunk in port and refloated several times during the war in the mediterranean.
