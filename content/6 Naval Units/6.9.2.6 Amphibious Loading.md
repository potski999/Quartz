#### 6.9.2.6 Amphibious Loading

There is an exception for all TFs that are meant to unload over the beach: Amphibious, Fast Transport, Barge and Landing Craft.

LCU’s can be “Combat Loaded” which essentially means they carry 3 times their normal amounts of ammo and fuel with them. Since ships can only load 1 type of item for bulk cargo this is a convenient way to bring enough ammo and fuel for offensive operations. Aviation Support LCU’s will try to pack up Aviation Fuel with them. Additional items will be taken out of the stores of the port as available.
