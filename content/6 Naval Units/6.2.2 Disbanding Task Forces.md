### 6.2.2 Disbanding Task Forces

A TF may disband itself if it is in a coastal hex or a friendly port. There is no limitation on the size of a TF that may disband (this is considered being at anchor). But you may not disband in hexes that contain bases where political relations are below 25. Ships at a friendly port may exist in the port separate from a task force in which case they can attempt to repair certain kinds of damage at the expense of additional vulnerability to enemy air and ship bombardment attacks.
