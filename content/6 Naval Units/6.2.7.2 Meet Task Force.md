#### 6.2.7.2 Meet Task Force

A TF given Meet Task Force orders will have a destination along the path of the targeted TF to meet.  The Tactical Map will be brought up.  Click on the TF icon that you want the current TF to meet, and the game will return to the TF Routing Screen.  If there is more than one TF in the hex, or you want to switch to another target TF, there are small arrow buttons below the name of the target TF on the routing screen.  These go forward or backwards through all the TFs in hex so you can pick the one you want.
