#### 6.13.6.1 Torpedo Supply

In *War in Spain 1936-39*, torpedoes are a pooled device of limited supply and build rate. Whenever a sub or surface ship (or aircraft) loads a torpedo, one is deducted from the pool. Keep an eye on your torpedo pools, so to avoid having none left when you need them the most.
