#### 6.9.3.1 Normal Unloading

Normal Unloading of a TF is governed by the same rates as loading a TF. And it is a function of Port Size. A TF will unload using the same rate calculations used for loading, modified by the presence of Naval Support and Port Damage, as described above in [[6.9.2.5 Port Load Rate Adjustments|section 6.9.2.5]].
