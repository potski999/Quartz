### 6.13.2 The Flag Bridge

The Flag Bridge is the Command Center of a Naval Engagement.  On the left are the Axis ships of the Selected Axis TF and on the right are the ships of the selected Allied TF.  The Center line shows the Various TFs of both sides as Icons.  On the bottom is data shown for the selected ships of either side.   The Message region between them will show the Messages of the more important events that happen during the battle.  As the Battle proceeds both the selected ships as well as selected TFs may change to show the ships that actions are executed upon.
