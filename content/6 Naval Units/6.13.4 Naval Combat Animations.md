### 6.13.4 Naval Combat Animations

The Naval Combat Animation screen will show surface actions as the ships fight them out.  Only detected enemy ships will be shown, some may only be radar contacts with no identification possible. As shots are traded, the display indicates by waterspouts, explosions and text messages detailing what is occurring on the screen.  If you do not wish to watch the entire battle and instead skip to the end Combat Summary, hit SPACE or ESC.  The combat results report will follow that details all participating combatants in the battle.  As well as the results of the combat.
