### 6.9.2 Loading Units

Loading initiates the actual load of Land and Air units.  Land and Air units are completely loaded during the allocation step.  There are 2 possible modes: normal loading (by left click) to distribute units to the ships of the TF or storing units on as few ships as possible (by right click). An attempt is made to allocate heavy devices to the appropriate ships. Tanks, vehicles, guns, or devices that contain animals are preferably allocated to LST, LSV, LCT, and AKA types so the heavy equipment does not need to be moved to shore by small Barges or Lighters.

>Note: In WEGO mode the loading of any unit will work like “Troop pickup” and be executed once the turn is processed.  This is due to several players executing the loading of the same units simultaneously.
