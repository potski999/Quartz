#### 6.15.2.2 Minefield Tenders

A special class of ships, Minefield Tenders (ACM) maintain the shape of minefields around bases. Each ACM can maintain (i.e. service and repair) up to 250 mines. ACM’s have a range of 20 NM or 4 Hexes. Multiple ACMs may be stacked in a base to protect more mines. **ACMs must be disbanded into a base to maintain mines there** – ACMs will not protect mines while in a TF at the base. ACM’s will burn fuel while at their base so make sure that you have fuel on hand.
