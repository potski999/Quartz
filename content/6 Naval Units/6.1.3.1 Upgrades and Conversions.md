#### 6.1.3.1 Upgrades and Conversions 

Upgrades and Conversions are similar in many ways but differ in one important respect. A conversion usually changes the nature of the ship. For example, a cargo ship becomes some type of support ship. Ships may Upgrade or Convert by clicking on the highlighted Upgrade or Convert field on the Ship Information Screen. Clicking on either field will bring up a screen that shows the characteristics of the ship being Upgraded or Converted to.
