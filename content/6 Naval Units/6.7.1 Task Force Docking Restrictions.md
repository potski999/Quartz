### 6.7.1 Task Force Docking Restrictions

There is a limitation on the size of a Ship that may “Dock” at Ports of various sizes, as well as a capacity of total tonnage that can dock at the same time.  There is no limitation on the size of a TF that may “Disband” at any Port.   Docking is done automatically for all ships of a TF that need to do so.  This, for example, means that escorts do not normally consume docking space unless they are in a TF that has escort type ships to load cargo.
