#### 6.15.3.1 Minelayers

Minelayers are ship classes “normally configured” to lay mines, such as CM, DM, CMc. Their normal mine ammunition is entered as weapons in the class device list, with a dispensing number, a dispensing mount size, and the number of mines carried as ammunition. Minelayers are listed in the Mine Warfare Task Force by Class Table in [[6.15.6 Mine Warfare Ship Classes Table|Section 6.15.6]] below.
