## 7.16 Air Unit Targets and Destinations

Targets  and  Destinations  are  Mission  objectives.  Targets  are  used in  Offensive  Missions  and  Destinations  in  Transport  Missions.  If  a Destination has been set for a unit with a Transport Mission, then that Destination will become the Destination for the Transport

Mission.  Without  a  Destination,  transports  will  not  fly  a  mission. Targets have no impact on aircraft flying Patrol, ASW or Training Missions.
