## 1.10 Technical Support

Should you have a technical problem with the game, the best way to get help is to post a note in the Technical Support sub-forum of the main game forum at http://www.matrixgames.com/forums. You’ll then hear back from either our Matrix Games Staff, the development team, or one of the many helpful players of the game. This method is usually the fastest way to get help. Alternatively, you can contact our Help Desk at http://www.matrixgames.com/support/ or support@matrixgames.com. Support requests will generally be answered within 24 hours, except on weekends or US/UK national holidays.

Thank you and enjoy your game!
