### 12.1.2 Ship Withdrawals

The computer will periodically check for ships being recalled (for other Theater Operations). The game rules can be set to have unit withdrawals or not. If withdrawals are active, then the ship withdrawals cannot be prevented, and other ships may take over the schedules in case the ship due to withdrawal is sunk.
