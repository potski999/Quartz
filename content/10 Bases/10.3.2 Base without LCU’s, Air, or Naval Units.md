### 10.3.2 Base without LCU’s, Air, or Naval Units

Present

This is an operational base that doesn’t contain a Port but does contain an empty Airfield. The airfield symbol will only display if an air unit and/or ship is located at the Base.

>Note: Depending on game settings, it is possible that Bases without Port or Airfield are shown as “dots” instead of using the nation flag.
