# 8 Ground Units

Ground  units  may  move  overland,  be  transported  by  sea  or  by  air. Parachute units can be airdropped onto enemy bases. Ground units can entrench  in  place  and  build  forts  to  increase  their  defensive  abilities. Combat units may assault enemy ground units and, in this way, capture enemy bases or positions.

Ground  units  represent  battalion  and

larger-sized  maneuver formations. These are made up of individual squads, guns, and vehicles, which are called devices. A typical infantry brigade has support troops, numerous  guns,  and  infantry  squads.  However,  the  orders  you  give will  be  to  the  maneuver  units,  (Battalions  and  larger  formations) rather than the individual squads and support troops. Maneuver units are represented on the map as ground unit icons. The support troops associated with the maneuver units will move with them.
