### 8.4.2 Movement along Railway Lines

LCU’s in Strategic Mode may only be given a Destination Hex that is connected to the unit’s current location by a rail line under your control. When both a railway and a road connect two adjacent hexes, LCU’s moving between the two hexes, in Strategic mode, will use the movement rate of the railroad.
