### 8.6.8 Ground Combat Aftermath

When ground combat is finished, a Combat Summary screen will be displayed. This details the location, type of attack made, composition of the attacking and defending forces, the assault odds for the attacker, whether the attacker captures the base, and any casualties that were suffered by both sides. Fog of War (FOW) can affect what is shown.
