### 8.8.1 Disruption

Disruption represents degradation of a LCU’s combat capabilities caused by combat and/or poor command control.  Also, dispersion of LCU’s components can cause disruption. Disruption may occur every turn depending on the state of the LCU. Any kind of attack against a ground unit will inflict disruption. Moving across a river hex side into a hex containing an enemy LCU’s will inflict disruption. Attack at odds less than 1:1, will cause a large amount of disruption to the attacking units. Disruption reduces firepower while increasing the damage taken during ground combat.

LCU’s remove Disruption every turn and will generally remove even high levels of Disruption within a few days. The recovery rate for disruption depends on whether or not the unit was involved in combat or moved during the current days turn. Best case it gets halved and then reduced by 1 to 10 points on average. This also depends on the leader admin skill.
