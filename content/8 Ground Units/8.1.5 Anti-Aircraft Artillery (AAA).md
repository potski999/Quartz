### 8.1.5 Anti-Aircraft Artillery (AAA)

Air Defense Units, also known as AAA, help to protect your bases and ground units from air attack. Without anti-aircraft guns, your bases and troops are vulnerable to air attack. However, AAA won’t survive long in a ground assault when directly engaged. They can, however, be very helpful in an AT role, as most AA guns fire projectiles at high velocities, such as the German 88’s.
