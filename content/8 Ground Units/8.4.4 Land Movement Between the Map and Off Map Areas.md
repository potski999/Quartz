### 8.4.4 Land Movement Between the Map and Off Map Areas

Some off-map areas have a land connection to the main map.  Travel along these connections can take longer than the visible distance would suggest. Land and air units may use normal land movement to move between the map and off-map areas. There is no special type of movement used. Only the distances per hex are different to make up for the abstracted way of showing the bases on map.
