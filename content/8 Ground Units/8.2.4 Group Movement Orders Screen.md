### 8.2.4 Group Movement Orders Screen

In the image below you have the unit movement screen:

![[8.2.4_Group_Movement.PNG|400]]

The column “Vehicles” has no function but tells the player if the unit is fit to move off road.

The column “selected” allows players to select units to be part of the group to move. None of the units are selected.

The column “Leading” allows the player to set the lead unit for the other units to follow. If no leader is selected the units will move at their own speed.

The column “mode” tells you what move mode the unit is in.

The button “Start movement” on the bottom right is clicked to confirm the move orders.
