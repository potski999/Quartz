## 19.22 Airborne Assaults

Normal combat troops can be used with transport aircraft to take over undefended airfields. Also, amphibious aircraft can be used to transport any combat unit to any undefended coastal hex. Defended hexes require an actual airdrop, or a landing using glider aircraft.
