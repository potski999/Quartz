### 24.6 Spanish Battleship Jaime I

The **Spanish Battleship Jaime I**.  She was the third and final ship of the España-class battleships, built for the Spanish Navy in the early 20th century.  Jaime I saw action during the Rif War, where she supported government forces by bombarding rebel positions along the Moroccan coast. At the outbreak of the Spanish Civil War, she remained loyal to the Republican Government. Tragically, in June 1937, she was destroyed by a catastrophic internal explosion while anchored at Cartagena, ending her service in dramatic fashion.
