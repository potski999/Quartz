## 7.4 Aircraft Sidebar

Below is the Aircraft Sidebar. The Sidebar makes it easier to see what is present at the base and to place some orders.

The  first  line  provides  you  with  the name  of  the  air  unit.  Next  is  the  number and  type  of  aircraft,  plus  the  military branch they are attached to. Below and to the left is a side view of the aircraft. To the right  of  the  picture  is  shown  the  aircrafts current  assigned  mission  and  two  of  its mission types.
