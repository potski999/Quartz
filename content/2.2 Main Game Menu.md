## 2.2 Main Game Menu

This is where War in Spain 1936-39 begins and where all your important game-defining decisions will be made, although some can be changed in  game.  From  here  all  game  parameters  are  defined  including  the selection  of  sides,  various  realism  options,  and  display  preferences.
