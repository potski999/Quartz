### 14.4.2 Convert Ship During Construction

The “Convert Ship” button opens this screen:

On the top and left you see data on the selected ship type. On the bottom you can select one of the valid conversions for this ship class (depending on game date and naval technology level) You can also see the time to finish the ship. If you convert early in construction, there will be no delay.  Converting a ship close to completion can cause an additional delay in the construction of that ship.
