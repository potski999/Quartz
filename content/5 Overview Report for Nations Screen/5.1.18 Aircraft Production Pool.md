### 5.1.18 Aircraft Production Pool

This screen provides detailed information of the available aircraft in the production pool for the selected nation.  Some aircraft might be in queue for refurbishing and shown in parenthesis here. This mostly happens to aircraft that are sent back to the pool from field units. Other information is the build rate, and number of aircraft built on the last day and in the current month. The screen also shows the date of availability for all airframes of this nation.
