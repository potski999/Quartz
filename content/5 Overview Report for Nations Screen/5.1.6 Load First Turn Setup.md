### 5.1.6 Load First Turn Setup

This allows you to import orders for your stock units from a special first turn save game. See [[5.1.7 Save As First Turn Setup|section 5.1.7]] below.

>Note: Certain orders that cause new units or commanders to be created, like splitting groups or loading LCUs on ships cannot be imported.
