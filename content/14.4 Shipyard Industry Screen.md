## 14.4 Shipyard Industry Screen

When a naval or merchant shipyard industry is selected, the following screen will be shown:

On the right-hand side, you will see a list of ships under construction at  this  base.  Ships  in  yellow  are  either  arriving  from  off  map,  or  this nation does not have on-map economy permitted. Selecting a “white” ship  by  mouse  click  will  show  some  arrow  buttons  that  allow  you  to adjust build speed. The speedup will cause an exponentially higher cost in heavy industry points but allows you to have certain ships available sooner.
