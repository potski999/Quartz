### 8.8.3 Fatigue

Fatigue represents wear and tear on troops and equipment, such as vehicles. as well as exhaustion and lack of sleep. Fatigued units will have reduced firepower.

Recovering Fatigue, however, takes far longer.  The recovery rate for fatigue is around 20% per day. Or a reduction of 1 to 10 points on average. This also depends on the leader admin skill and the unit activity level.  In other words, if the LCU is moving or in combat it makes it harder to reduce fatigue.
