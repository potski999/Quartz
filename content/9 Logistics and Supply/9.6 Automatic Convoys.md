## 9.6 Automatic Convoys

Automatic Convoy TF’s can be built to send supplies and other materials to ports around the map. These types of TF’s are discussed in [[6.1.2.2.6 Trade and Cargo Routes (Auto-Convoys)|section 6.1.2.2.6 Trade and cargo routes]].
