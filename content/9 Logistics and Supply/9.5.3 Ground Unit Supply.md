### 9.5.3 Ground Unit Supply

When tracing a supply path for movement of ground units or overland movement of supplies, travel time is calculated for both foot and truck movement types.  This is determined by tracing a path from the base transferring supplies or the ground unit moving to the destination. Both types of logistics will be used separately, but in special conditions they team up. See [[9.1 Unit Logistics Screen|section 9.1]]. The exact data for path and capacity can be seen from the ground unit screen when clicking on the logistics Cap text line in the lower left of the Unit Logistics Screen.
