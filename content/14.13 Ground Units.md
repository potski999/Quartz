## 14.13 Ground Units

When production is turned on and the owning nation is defined to have on-map economy, all ground units arriving as reinforcements must be flushed out with new elements from the production system. If the production system cannot produce enough weapons to fill out the entire TOE of a new ground unit, then the unit will be placed on the map at what is available of its TOE.  If there is not enough manpower in the pool, then the GNP is automatically lowered by drafting men into the armed forces.
