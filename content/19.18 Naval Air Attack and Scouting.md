## 19.18 Naval Air Attack and Scouting

Air groups that are set for naval attack will revert from their scouting assignment towards the main mission if a target is found.

For example, a USN Dive Bomber group set to 50% attack and 50% scout, which is normal for the USN, will change to 100% attack in case a target is known at the time of the scout phase, these aircraft will perform a naval attack with the main mission instead. It is possible to detect an enemy with the morning scouting sorties and have all aircraft go in for attack in the afternoon sorties.
