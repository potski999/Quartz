## 7.24 Weather and Aborted Missions

Missions can be aborted after all preparations have been made, due to bad weather over the air unit’s base or over the intended target. Hexes affected by bad weather, winds of Beaufort 6 and higher, block any air units from launching an airstrike from the hex, and it blocks any target in the hex from being attacked if winds are Beaufort 9 and higher. For details on the Beaufort Scale see [[12.1.1 Task Force Leaders|section 12.1.1]].

Very low temperatures (below – 20 °C) can also prevent the launching of any aircraft on strike missions, although a very low temperature will not prevent a strike from hitting a hex. A line of bad weather will not stop an airstrike flying through the hex from a good weather hex to a good weather hex. A no-fly symbol (a cloud) will appear on the tactical map if the Show Weather Effect option is selected.
