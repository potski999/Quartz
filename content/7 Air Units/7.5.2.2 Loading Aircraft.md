#### 7.5.2.2 Loading Aircraft 

There are 2 ways to load air units on a ship.
- As cargo (have a TF load the air units), or
- As an active air unit (by moving the group onto a capable ship).

Any Float plane or Float fighter may be based on a Battleship, Heavy Cruiser, CS, or other non-carrier type ship that has the capacity to carry aircraft. Float planes, float fighters, and some patrol planes can be based on friendly airfields, including those that are size 0 if they are on a coastal hex.
