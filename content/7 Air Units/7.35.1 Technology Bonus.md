### 7.35.1 Technology Bonus

The Technology Bonus is how good a leader or pilot is in advanced A2A tactics, such as vertical warfare. It ranges from 0 to 100% and generally improves pilot performance. Certain leaders and pilots may be granted a technology bonus at a rate of 3% for every point over 70 air experience. Even if no technology bonus is available for that nation. It generally is an ability on the national level, but leaders and pilots can also provide it based on their skill.
