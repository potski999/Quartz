## 7.33 Air Unit Detachments

Some air units start the game as detachments to a parent air unit. These detachments count against the total aircraft of the parent air unit, as do the pilots.  On the parent air unit screen, you will be shown the maximum aircraft size of the air unit less the number of detachments. There is a “Unit OOB” button which will show the air units connected to the parent air unit.
