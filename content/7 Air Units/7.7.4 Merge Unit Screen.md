### 7.7.4 Merge Unit Screen

Below is the merge units screen followed by an explanation:

![[7.7.4_Marge_Unit_Screen.PNG|400]]

On top of the screen is the data for the current air group. You will see information on the current and maximum size, the currently active (able to take-off) aircraft and the number of pilots.

Listed below are possible groups for exchanging airframes. You can draw or send airframes to the selected group by the arrows on the right side. Draw 10, 5 or 1 by the arrows pointing to left and send 1, 5 or 10 by use of the arrows pointing to right. Clicking the right mouse button will move the aircraft along with a pilot, if possible.
