## 7.14 Air HQs and Air Missions

Air HQ’s will help air operations within their Operational Radius. The air skill of the HQ leader will give certain combat benefits to attached groups. Skill levels above 70 are especially valuable. The same kind of bonus is given to group leaders, but only for their group. The Command Radius of an HQ is displayed on its Ground Unit Information screen in the top left corner.
