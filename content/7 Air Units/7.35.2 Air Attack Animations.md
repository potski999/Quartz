### 7.35.2 Air Attack Animations

**Air attack animations are designed to provide the player with on-the** - spot information as to what is happening during a raid. This information is usually pretty accurate. The animations can be skipped by pressing either space or escape. In naval attacks the first keystroke will advance to the attack on the ships.
