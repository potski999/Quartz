### 7.5.2 Patrol and Float Aircraft

Float planes can sometimes not return to their ship. This applies mostly to CAM ships and some vessels that were designed to have their aircraft return to shore bases after the mission. An aircraft cannot return to a non-carrier ship if it’s runway need is greater than the ship’s runway. For launch, the runway size of the ship is compared to the runway need of the airframe. If the runway is smaller than 50% of the runway needed, the aircraft cannot take off. If the runway is between 50% and 100% of the need, the aircraft can only launch with reduced load.

Aircraft that are not fit for operating on a given ship normally can only be loaded on ship in a port. This applies to aircraft being loaded on combat vessels or CAM ships.
