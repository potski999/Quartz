## 7.34 Air Combat

Air combat in War in Spain 1936-39 occurs when opposing aircraft meet. This may happen in the target hex of an air strike or along the approach leg of the inbound air strike.  When an air strike has been launched, the Tactical Map will center on the hex being attacked.  As they engage in combat, the results will be displayed.
