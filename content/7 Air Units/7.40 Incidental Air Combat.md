## 7.40 Incidental Air Combat

Aircraft flying into a hex that contains enemy aircraft may, at any point of combat resolution, be caught up in an air battle in that hex.  Thus, while witnessing one set of aircraft fighting each other, other aircraft, not with that set, may end up participating and becoming casualties in the air-to-air combat.
