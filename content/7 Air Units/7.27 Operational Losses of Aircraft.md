## 7.27 Operational Losses of Aircraft

Operational Losses (or Op Losses) occur due to accidents or other events that are not necessarily a direct result of being shot at by the enemy. These losses occur to squadrons based on several factors, including the range of the Mission, the experience of the pilot, and if the aircraft is flying from a ship. The result of ops losses ranges from damage during landing to complete loss of a aircraft and it’s pilot.  Op losses include planes lost returning to base and planes crashing, planes destroyed or damaged on take-off and/or landing, and pilots as a result getting killed, wounded, or captured.

As with all other losses, replacements for an air group can be accomplished either automatically or manually.  See [[16.4 Air Unit Replacements|section 16.4]] for details.
