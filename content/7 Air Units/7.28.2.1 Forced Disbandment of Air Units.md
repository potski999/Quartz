#### 7.28.2.1 Forced Disbandment of Air Units

An air unit subject to forced disbandment can be identified by a line of text in the air unit panel telling the date of disbandment. Thus, forced disbandment primarily affects the size of the air force the player can field rather than the raw number of pilots and aircraft.
