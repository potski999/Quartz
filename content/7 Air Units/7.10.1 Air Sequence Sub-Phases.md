### 7.10.1 Air Sequence Sub-Phases

There are sub-phases executed in preparation for every combat phase. They are:

- CAP / LRCAP sorties at 0500, 1300 and 1900.  These can cause repeated small skirmishes over hexes that have CAP of both sides present.
- Scout and recon are calculated at 0600, 1400 and 2100. CAP and LRCAP try to interdict these missions.
- Air Attack Phases are done at 0800, 1600 and 2200. Any airstrikes by attack aircraft are executed at these times.
