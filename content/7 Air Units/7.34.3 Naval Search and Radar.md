### 7.34.3 Naval Search and Radar

Patrol aircraft may be detected by radar, causing CAP to intercept way out of visual range. Since patrols are required to move into visual range of any target at sea, to get a DL on it, radar can be a serious obstacle in acquiring a target. Especially a naval task force. So, unless the scout aircraft can outrun or outgun the CAP, you will be provided with a minimum DL on any TF that may be present in the target hex.
