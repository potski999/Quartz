## 7.10 Air Sequence of Play

Air operations take place during the Air Ops segments of AM, PM, and Night Resolution phases. The game, based on the orders given to the air units by you, determines these operations. Only air units that have been given “Night Operations” status will conduct operations during the night. The launching of Combat Air Patrols (CAP), launching of strikes, resolution of strikes, and return of aircraft occurs in any of the phases.
