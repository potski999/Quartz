## 3.3 WEGO Team Games

WEGO Team Games allow for more than one player per side.  See [[3.3.1 WEGO Team Games Setup|section 3.3.1]] below for a complete explanation of WEGO Team Play.

>Note: When starting a WEGO Team Game, you will be prompted to enter a password.  As mentioned above, make sure that the password you choose is the one that you want as once entered you can’t change it.
