## 18.4 General Rules on Withdrawing and Disbanding

There is no way to prevent unit withdrawal. Upon withdrawing, a unit will immediately be removed from game since the player is no longer in command of or responsible for this unit. Air and ground units due to withdraw cannot be disbanded manually.
