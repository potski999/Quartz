## 6.6 Operation Points

Operation  Points  (or  OPs)  reflect  the  time  spent  on  refueling, replenishing  ammo,  and  loading/unloading  of  cargo.  These  actions reduce  the  movement  of  a  TF  during  a  Resolution  Phase.  During an  Orders  Phase,  if  a  TF  refuels  or  is  ordered  to  load  troops,  the  TF Information  Screen  will  reflect  the  amount  of  time  already  used  in Operation Points. Every TF has 24 Operation Points per day.

Thus,  if  a  TF  refuels  and  the  display  shows  a  ship  has  used  10 Operation Points, 10 of 24 hours of the day have been expended. This means the Task Forces cannot move or act for 10 hours. The port may also receive a number of ops points (counted in minutes) indicating it cannot serve any ships until this time has passed.
