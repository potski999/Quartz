## 14.9 Fuel and Refineries

Fuel represents the types of refined fuel oils used for fueling ships. As well as those products refined from oil that are required to operate industrial centers.

Fuel points are generated by refinery centers.  Fuel Points are required inputs for heavy industry centers.  They are also required to fuel most ships, ground units and aircraft. Aircraft and ships and LCU use different kinds of fuel each.
