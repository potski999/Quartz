## 14.1 Production Buttons

When a location is selected, any Production ability it has will be reflected in the bottom row of buttons on the display. For example:

![[14.1_Production_Buttons.PNG|400]]

Madrid has several buttons along the bottom of the screen representing the production facilities it has. The Production Buttons in the above example are from left to right:

- Manpower,
- Light Industry,
- Railroad Depot,
- Resources

Clicking on one of the buttons will bring up the Location Industry screen. See [[14.1.1 Location Industry Screen|section 14.1.1]] below.
