#### 4.8.1.1 Coal and Coal Mines

Coal is one of the primary energy sources that keeps industry running. You will find many industries require Coal to run. Some ships may also be coal fired and consume it for fuel.

Coal is produced by Coal Mines. These are located in base hexes, and each day produce 1 ton of Coal per industry center and that Coal goes into storage at that location.
