#### 14.6.1.1 Aircraft Factory Alterations

Players may convert an aircraft factory to create a different kind of aircraft.  This change can cause a reduction in the number of aircraft factories, and damage to the remaining aircraft factories.  The only exception is an upgrade to the defined upgrade of the current aircraft.

The reduction in output capacity depends on the differences in cost of the 2 aircraft types involved.
