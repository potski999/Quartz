# 11 Detection and Spotting of Units

Detecting the enemy is imperative in War in Spain 1936-39. But even more important is the accuracy of the information that you receive. The Detection Level (DL) is a measure of telling how accurate the information is.  It ranges from 0 to 20 normally and is commonly hidden, except for TFs and Bases. For TFs, the DL is telling how precise the position of the TF can be guessed. TF spotting has a second value equal to the quality of the spotter, normally the naval search skill of a pilot or lookout on a ship. This second value ranges from 0 to 100 and indicates how accurate the information on the contents of the TF will be. What can’t be seen can’t be destroyed, and reconnaissance is the eyes and ears of the wise commander.
