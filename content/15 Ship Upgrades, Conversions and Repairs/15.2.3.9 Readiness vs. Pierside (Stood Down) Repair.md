#### 15.2.3.9 Readiness vs. Pierside (Stood Down) Repair

Readiness and Pierside (stood down) repairs are calculated substantially the same way, except for certain factors in the calculations. A ship that is being repaired while standing down will get better calculation results and repair damage more quickly because it does not have to be kept constantly ready-for-action.  In general, for Readiness repairs, Port Assist and Naval Support are approximately 50% as effective.  Major damage, even small amounts of it, can only be fixed in Pierside Repair.
