#### 15.2.3.2. Repair Ship Repair

Several different types of Repair Ships are available to assist in repair of different types of vessels. The extent of damage that can be repaired by Repair Ships depends on the “kind” of Repair Ship; for example, an ARD may only repair floatation damage but may repair Major floatation damage.  ARs and other types may NOT repair severe Major damage for larger ships. Small amounts of major damage, up to 5 points, may be fixed, depending on the ability (size) of the AR / ARD.  They can repair major damage to small craft, like PTs and barges. Ships may use the assistance of Repair Ships in two ways: at anchor, or at dockside. In either mode, the Repair Ships available to repair different warship types are as follows:
