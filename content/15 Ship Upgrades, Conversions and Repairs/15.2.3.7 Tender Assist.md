#### 15.2.3.7 Tender Assist

If any Tenders capable of providing repair services are present in port, the Tenders can contribute 500 repair points. Tenders are also allowed to repair devices and weapons on the ships they can support but may be very slow at this in case of more potent weapons. They can utilize the other repair point sources on top of their own for this. The actual points that can be spent on any ship again depends on ship size and number of ships under repair.
