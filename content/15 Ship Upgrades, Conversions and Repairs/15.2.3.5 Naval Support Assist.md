#### 15.2.3.5 Naval Support Assist

Naval Support squads can assist in repairing damage to ships at pierside, either in the stood down Pierside mode, or normal Readiness pierside mode, and for ships assigned to AR Repair. Naval Support generates 1 Repair Point each. The amount that can and will be allocated to each ship depends on the size of the ship (it is not possible to have 1000 men contribute to fixing a Barge) and the number of ships under repair as well as the individual priority mode.
