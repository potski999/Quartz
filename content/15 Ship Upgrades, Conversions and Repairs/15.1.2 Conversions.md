### 15.1.2 Conversions

Conversions usually change the purpose of a ship.  For example, converting a standard xAK into an AKE or AE munitions ship. Conversions always need to be done at a Base with a Repair Shipyard large enough to accommodate the ship and the ship will be forced to convert under “Repair Shipyard Mode” for the duration of the work.
