### 15.2.4 Weapon Repair Costs and Requirements

Weapon repair costs are figured by the size of the “mount in barrels x (Device Armor + Device Penetration)” Weapons can be repaired if the Device Penetration is “lower or equal to Port size x 100”.  Alternately a Tender can permit repairs to weapons for ship types it can support. Repair Yard mode can also repair weapons.
