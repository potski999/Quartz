## 15.1 Ship Upgrades and Conversions

Upgrades and Conversions are similar in many ways but differ in one important respect. Conversion changes the type of a ship, for example a cargo ship into an auxiliary ship of some kind.  Conversions also always need to be done at a Base with a Repair Shipyard large enough to accommodate the ship. Ships may Upgrade or Convert by clicking on the highlight Upgrade or Convert field on the Ship Information Screen.  Clicking on either field will bring up a screen that shows the characteristics of the ship being Upgraded or Converted to.  There is an additional conversion option available, an “AK Carry Troops (AK- t)” option. This converts some of a ships bulk cargo space to be more habitable for troops.
